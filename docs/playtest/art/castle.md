# Art and atmosphere playtest: Briarholt Castle

Tester: AI persona "art and atmosphere critic", Kingdom test, day 1 from 6:00 am to 2:00 am, about 125 harness commands.
Screenshots: `C:/Users/sebas/AppData/Local/Temp/claude/C--Users-sebas-Desktop-emberhollow/fa185991-22ce-4f9b-bebc-32e568c2d357/scratchpad/playtest/a1_*.png` (named below as `a1_NN`).

Route: the herald's procession on the farm, Elias at the museum, Ashcombe at the manor, the south-east gap, the outer road, the gate, the courtyard, the keep (ground floor, upper floor, cellars), the courtyard again at dusk (7-8 pm) and at night (1:30-2 am).

**I could not buy the deed.** Isolde's step asks for **30,000g**. Kingdom test starts at 25,000, plus 1,500 from Isolde, so I had 26,500. The brief said 22,000 would be enough. Because I never owned the castle, I never saw the owned court (guards, cook, maids, knights, nobles, the bard, diners in the hall). This report covers the castle **before ownership**, which is what every player sees first.

## Summary: does it feel like a royal castle? **5.5 / 10**

The **set dressing is good**:
- the keep facade, with round towers, purple stag banners, crenellations and ivy;
- a proper twin-towered gate;
- a fountain plaza with lamp posts and banner poles;
- iron-framed glasshouses;
- a marble throne room with a dais, a purple velvet throne, a tapestry of the castle and gilded candelabras.

The **scale and life are missing**:
- The grounds are mostly empty lawn.
- The walls are low and, on the long north-south runs, read as flat stone paths.
- The keep's rooms are huge marble or plank floors with a handful of props in each.
- On day 1 the only people on the whole estate are Wenna and Aldous, who end up standing on top of each other by the keep door.

It reads as a **beautiful, abandoned estate**. That fits the story before you buy the castle, but even its dressing is sparse.

## Art consistency and beauty

### Lovely
- **The keep facade** (`a1_37`, `a1_62`): round flanking towers, banners with the stag, a crest over a deep arched door with a portcullis edge, a string course, and ivy climbing the left tower. It matches the town buildings' style and is the best piece on the map.
- **The south wall and gatehouse** (`a1_33`, `a1_34`): crenellations, conical slate tower roofs, arrow slits and a little portcullis arch. The towers are well shaded.
- **The glasshouses** (`a1_31`, `a1_60`): iron frames with citrus trees showing through the glass. Gorgeous, and nothing else in the game is like them.
- **The throne room** (`a1_43`, `a1_49`, `a1_55`): purple runner, dais steps, a velvet throne with gold finials, a tapestry of Briarholt, portraits and candelabras. Sitting on the throne gives "You shouldn't. Not yet.", a great touch.
- **The chapel's stained glass** (`a1_48`) and the great hall's long tables with roast fowl, candles and plates (`a1_41`).
- **The night grade** (`a1_80`, `a1_81`): a deep blue-violet, with the lamp-post lanterns as small orange squares. Very pretty, and the dusk amber at 7 pm is lovely too (`a1_60`, `a1_61`).

### Off-style, empty, clipping or mis-sorted
1. **The north-south curtain walls read as paths, not walls** (`a1_29`, `a1_31`, `a1_60`).
   - Seen from the outer road, the west wall is a 2-tile strip of grey blocks lying flat on the grass, with no face, no height and no shadow.
   - The south wall right next to it has a full face and crenellations, so the two runs don't match.
   - The shadow added for east-facing runs isn't enough. They need battlements along the top edge and a visible inner or outer face.
2. **The walls are too low for a royal castle.** The south wall face is about 2.5 tiles. The player's head reaches a third of it, and the gate arch is barely taller than a person. Royal curtain walls should dwarf the player: 4-5 tiles, a taller gatehouse with a walkway over the arch.
3. **The interior walls between rooms are black voids** (`a1_50`, `a1_53`, `a1_54`, `a1_76`).
   - East-west walls get a nice brick face.
   - North-south walls are pure black strips with a brown edge, and the doorways show odd square brick stubs (`a1_48`, `a1_49`).
   - It looks unfinished next to the brick faces.
4. **Player-over-wall clipping.** The player can stand on the brick face of a north wall: in the kitchen (`a1_53`), the dungeon (`a1_77`) and the wine cellar, where he is drawn over the shelf (`a1_78`).
5. **NPC overlap.**
   - The player and Isolde occupy the same spot while talking; her dress pokes out above his helmet (`a1_57`, `a1_58`).
   - Aldous and Wenna overlap at the door (`a1_65`).
   - On the farm, the player stood inside a knight of the procession, and the first RMB talked to the knight instead of the herald (`a1_04`, `a1_05`).
6. **The player sprite turns into a pale grey silhouette** for long stretches (`a1_07`, `a1_09`, `a1_10`, `a1_16`, `a1_17`), even with nothing covering him.
   - On the open farm and in the village plaza the see-through overlay seems to stay on.
   - It makes the hero look like a ghost and kills the colour of the iron armour.
7. **The rooms are huge and sparse.**
   - The **solar** (`a1_69`) is a big plank room with one desk, two chairs, a rug and a harp.
   - The **royal bedchamber** (`a1_70`) has a fine four-poster but otherwise one wardrobe, one dresser and a rug in a room about 16 tiles wide.
   - The **wine cellar, dungeon and crypt** (`a1_72`, `a1_74`, `a1_76`) are the same pale flagstone with the same lighting as the kitchen. The cellars aren't dim, the dungeon has no straw, chains or manacles, and the crypt's tombs are tiny flat slabs with no effigies, candles or gloom.
   - The **great hall** has a single row of tables per side and bare walls (`a1_42`).
   - Every ground-floor room uses the same cream and grey chequered marble (throne room, chapel, hall, entrance), so the floors run together.
8. **"The great hearth" is a small stone stove-fireplace** (`a1_51`, `a1_52`). It should be a wall-wide hearth with a spit, cauldron and chimney hood.
9. **The keep windows never light up at night.** At 8:40 pm and at 1:30-2 am the keep's windows are dark blue (`a1_65`, `a1_80`, `a1_81`), although Aldous and the "warm kitchen" story say people live there. The bedchamber's window decals also show a daytime blue sky at 10 pm (`a1_70`).
10. **The fountain is the village fountain reused** (`a1_36`), and its water barely reads as moving. A royal plaza deserves its own fountain (a stag statue, a two-tier basin).
11. **The portraits are one design in two colours, repeated** (`a1_69`, `a1_49`): red-robed and purple-robed, the same face every time.
12. **Lord Ashcombe's manor** is a cottage the same size as the neighbouring houses (`a1_25`). The man who held Briarholt's keys for 40 years should live somewhere grander.
13. **Room labels sit under the tracker arrow** ("GREAT HALL", "THRONE ROOM", `a1_41`, `a1_43`). The label is half hidden.
14. **The tracker arrow is wrong or frozen.**
    - "Elias · 32 m" kept pointing up-left while I walked across the village (`a1_16`-`a1_18`); the museum was down-right.
    - In the keep, "Search here" pointed straight up at a wall (`a1_44`).
    - Not an art problem, but it drags you away from the castle's sights.

## Atmosphere (sound, light, night)

- **Sound:** not checked (the harness mutes audio).
- **Light:**
  - The time-of-day grade is the highlight: warm amber dusk, then a rich indigo night with lit lanterns.
  - But no castle windows light up, there are no torches or braziers on the walls or by the gate, and the candelabras inside are never lit (no flame pixels at 10 pm).
  - Interiors keep the same flat brightness on all three floors. The crypt is as bright as the throne room.
- **Night:** the grounds are empty at night apart from two people stuck at the door. There are no guards with lanterns, no lit gatehouse and no glow from the kitchen. It's pretty, but it isn't lived in.

## Concrete ideas to make it feel more royal (ranked)

1. **Taller walls with readable height on every side.** Give north-south runs a battlemented top edge plus a visible face or shadow, raise the curtain to 4+ tiles, and make a real gatehouse: a 2-storey arch, a portcullis visibly raised, wooden gates and torches either side. This is the one change that makes it look like a fortress from the road.
2. **Light the castle at night.** Lit keep windows (`meta.win`), braziers at the gate and keep door, wall sconces with flames inside, lit candelabras, and a glow in the kitchen hearth. Add a slightly dimmer grade for the cellars and crypt.
3. **Life before ownership.**
   - A few staff doing things: Wenna kneeling in the beds, Aldous walking the hall, a stable boy, a cat at the hearth.
   - Ambient creatures: crows on the battlements, rats in the cellar, doves at the fountain.
   - Steam from the kitchen pot.
4. **Fill and differentiate the rooms.**
   - Floors: red carpet runners, fur rugs in the bedchamber, rush-strewn flagstones in the hall, dark slate in the crypt.
   - Walls: shields and crossed swords in the hall and armoury, tapestries, a hanging chandelier sprite over the throne.
   - A canopy or cloth of estate behind the throne.
   - Bookcases filling the library walls.
   - Effigy tombs and candles in the crypt; straw, chains and a skeleton in the cells.
   - A wall-wide great hearth in the kitchen with a spit and a hanging game rack.
5. **Texture the north-south interior walls** (brick face or a top-down wall cap) instead of black, and stop the player standing on wall faces.
6. **A unique royal fountain** (a stag on a plinth, a two-tier basin with animated jets) and a statue of King Oswin or Margit in the plaza.
7. **More pennants and heraldry outside:** flags on every tower roof (one tower already has one), banners hanging from the curtain wall, and a heraldic shield over the gate.
8. **Varied portraits**: Oswin, Margit, Isolde as a child, Aldren. Use the actual cast's faces.
9. **A grander Ashcombe manor**, at least the size of the museum, with the stag crest.

## Top 5 fixes

1. **The deed price and the Kingdom test gold don't match.** The step costs 30,000g and the test gives 25,000 (+1,500), so the owned court can't be reached in the test. Either give the test 35,000g or lower the price.
2. **North-south curtain walls look like flat paths**, and the walls are too low. Give them a battlement top and a face or shadow, and raise them.
3. **No lit windows, torches or candles at night** at the keep and gate; the cellars and crypt aren't dimmed.
4. **Interior north-south walls are black voids**, and the player clips over north wall faces in the kitchen and cellars.
5. **The player is drawn as a pale silhouette for long stretches outdoors**, and NPCs and the player stack on the same tiles (Isolde, Aldous with Wenna, the knight on the farm).
