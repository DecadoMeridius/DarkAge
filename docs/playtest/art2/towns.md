# Art & atmosphere: the realm's towns, round 2

Persona: the art and atmosphere critic. Kingdom test, Spring 1 06:00 to Spring 2 10:10, about 130 harness commands. Screenshots are `b2_<n>.png` in the playtest scratchpad.

Overall this is a clear step up. Greyhelm and Ashford now each have a clear identity. The roads are single, clean strips and the town walls with parapets read well. The weak spots are interiors that all look the same, roads that stop at town gates, and a few interaction snags.

## Roads and terrain now

- **One clean road per route: mostly yes.** From the farm's north exit to Greyhelm, every road I followed was a single dirt strip with T-junctions (`b2_32`, `b2_33`, `b2_36`). There were no braids. The bridges in Whisperwood are straight plank bridges on a straight road (`b2_66`). This is much better than last round.
- **One braid is left:** in the snowy north there are two parallel east-west roads about 4 tiles apart, joined by a third (`b2_37`). It reads as a grid again.
- **Cliffs:** I was never trapped. The meadow and hills still have lots of terraced, boxy cliff blocks with straight right-angle steps (`b2_30`, `b2_31`, `b2_35`). Walking around them is fine now, but they look like Tetris pieces rather than land. Several cliff blocks sit right against the road edge (`b2_32`, the road runs along a cliff face), which is fine.
- **Roads that stop at gates:**
  - Kingsbridge's west gate has a cobble stub of 3-4 tiles, then grass (`b2_65`). The signpost there says "Ashford ←", but there is no road west. The real Ashford road leaves from somewhere else.
  - Ashford's west exit has the same cobble stub into forest (`b2_69`).
  - Gates should only exist where a road leaves, or the stub should join the road.
- **Signposts:** labels are readable and every gate has one ("Kingsbridge ↓ · Ashford ↙ · Saltmere ↘ · Emberhollow ↓" at Greyhelm, `b2_43`).
  - The arrows are coarse, and "Saltmere →" from Kingsbridge's west gate pointed into a field.
  - "to Hollowmere Farm" floats over the player's head everywhere in the Wilds (`b2_31` onward). It is the tracker label, but it covers the art in every screenshot.
- **Off-road walking:** RMB on a far point paths straight across the land and leaves the road, so I lost the Ashford→Kingsbridge road and wandered a meadow (`b2_78`). This is a player problem more than an art one, but "follow the road" isn't natural with click-to-move.
- **Danger:**
  - The road north crosses the Frost Giant's and Bandit Warlord's arenas, and the boss bar popped up both times (`b2_38`, `b2_42`).
  - Snow wolves and wisps took me from 300 to 10 HP on the main road to Greyhelm (`b2_40`), and I arrived at Greyhelm's gate with 8 HP.
  - The roads between towns don't feel "gentle". Kingsbridge's surroundings are fine.

## Per town

### Greyhelm (`b2_43`-`b2_57`)
- **Seen:**
  - Crenellated sandstone walls with blue-roofed corner towers, a gatehouse arch, guards at the gate and lamp posts lining the cobbled cross street.
  - Turf-roofed stone longhouses and slate-roofed halls.
  - A mine mouth with a timber door, rails curving out, ore carts on the rails, slag heaps and coal piles by the forge.
  - A red-roofed well, a central rune waystone and crates.
  - Knights on patrol walk through.
  - Lit windows at dusk (`b2_50`). The night blue grade with warm windows is lovely (`b2_53`).
- **Interiors:** the Longhouse (`b2_48`, banner, bookshelves, fireplace, rug, the elder at a desk) and The Iron Kettle inn (`b2_51`, 9 patrons in the evening). Good.
- **Missing:**
  - The south half (four turf-roof blocks along the wall, `b2_45`) is just roofs with no fronts or doors. They read as hedges.
  - Nobody works the mine or pushes a cart.
  - No smoke from the chimneys.
  - Streets empty out completely by 21:00 (`b2_54`).
  - The wall inside the town is the same sandstone as Kingsbridge. A darker granite would suit "grey helm".

### Kingsbridge (`b2_58`-`b2_65`, `b2_81`)
- **Seen:**
  - A plaza with a tiered fountain, four striped market stalls and benches.
  - A temple with a portico, an emporium with a teal roof and a blue awning, and half-timbered houses with red and blue roofs.
  - Trees between blocks, a lake outside the east wall and guards at the gates.
- **Interiors:** the inn (`b2_61`) had 9 patrons at 22:40 and a patron line ("Sit down, friend..."). The smithy (`b2_80`) is lovely: forge hood, coal, weapon rack, quench barrel, anvil.
- **Missing:**
  - At night the streets were empty (only guards). In the rain at 10:00 there were only about 5 people. For a capital it feels quiet.
  - No market noise: vendors stand at stalls but nobody browses.
  - The east and west walls are a thin column of masonry with a single tower (`b2_81`, `b2_65`), much thinner than the tall south wall with its parapet. The side gates are just gaps with no gatehouse.

### Ashford (`b2_67`-`b2_77`)
- **Seen:**
  - Dark log cabins with shingle roofs, cream plaster cottages with green shutters, and a tall plank barn with a sign.
  - Log piles and brick-like stacks of cut timber, stumps, a red well and a waystone.
  - Lamp posts at the crossroads, a split-rail fence round the town, and a cobbled road leaving east and west.
  - Rain and warm windows at night (`b2_68`). By morning: 6-8 townsfolk, two watchmen with a nice line ("You're not strange. Probably.").
- **Missing:**
  - Nothing says "sawmill": no saw bench, no sawdust, no logs on trestles, no water wheel.
  - Nobody chops wood.
  - The tavern (`b2_70`) is the same room as the Iron Kettle (bookshelves, banner, fireplace, two barrels, rug and table), only with plank walls. It was empty and had no keeper at 01:40, while the brief says inns are open all night.

### Saltmere
Not reached. On the map it is a small box far east, only connected by a long road (`b2_79`).

## Art

**Lovely:**
- Greyhelm's mine corner (`b2_44`).
- Night grade plus lit windows over the cobbles (`b2_53`, `b2_58`).
- Ashford in rain (`b2_73`).
- Kingsbridge plaza and fountain (`b2_58`).
- The smithy interior (`b2_80`).
- The Frost Giant and the snowy pines (`b2_38`).
- The Wilds mine entrance on its crossroads (`b2_35`).
- The parapeted walls with conical-roof towers (`b2_43`).

**Off-style and clipping:**
1. Every inn and tavern interior is the same layout and furniture (`b2_51`, `b2_61`, `b2_70`). Only the wall texture changes. Bookshelves in a woodcutters' tavern feel wrong.
2. Two patrons stand in front of the bar counter, so I couldn't reach the Kingsbridge innkeeper. I talked to a patron instead, then clipped onto the counter's right end (`b2_63`, `b2_64`).
3. The player overlaps the Ashford waystone, standing on its top (`b2_76`).
4. Greyhelm's south row: hip roofs with no visible front, so they read as green hedges (`b2_45`).
5. Terraced cliff plateaus are perfectly rectilinear with stair-step corners (`b2_30`, `b2_31`). They look generated, not natural.
6. The "to Hollowmere Farm" tracker label is always on screen in the Wilds.
7. Interiors are shown as a small room in a big black screen, with the room name drawn half over the top wall (`b2_48`). The name is legible, but the name on the wall cuts into the art.
8. The Kingsbridge inn showed no title on entry, while the Greyhelm rooms did.

## Concrete ideas, ranked

1. Give each inn its own room: a smoky long hall with antlers and log benches for Ashford, nets, a ship's wheel and lanterns for Saltmere, a stone vault with mining lamps for Greyhelm, and a tiled hall with a gallery for Kingsbridge.
2. Join each town's gates to the actual road network, or remove gates that lead nowhere (Kingsbridge west, Ashford west).
3. Ashford: a real sawmill yard (saw pit, trestles, sawdust heap, cart of logs), plus a woodcutter NPC chopping on a timer.
4. Greyhelm: a miner NPC pushing a cart along the rails, chimney smoke, and darker granite walls.
5. Kingsbridge: more townsfolk at night (a watchman with a lantern, tavern-goers), shoppers moving between stalls by day, and full-height east and west walls with gatehouses.
6. A "follow road" walk mode, or make RMB pathing prefer road tiles, so long trips stay on the road.
7. Round off the corners of cliff plateaus and break them up with boulders and slopes.
8. Make the roads between towns safer (a patrol presence, fewer snow wolves right on the road), or move the boss arenas off the main road.

## Top 5 fixes

1. **Inn counters:** keep patrons out of the tiles in front of the counter so the keeper can be reached (Kingsbridge inn, `b2_63`).
2. **The Ashford waystone wouldn't open** with E or RMB from any side I tried (four tries, `b2_76`/`b2_77`), and the player stood on top of it. Greyhelm's needed a sideways nudge before it opened. Make waystones react from any neighbouring tile, like story objects.
3. **The Ashford tavern was empty with no keeper at 01:40**, although inns are meant to be open all night.
4. **Dead-end gates and road stubs:** Kingsbridge west and Ashford west, plus the signpost pointing "Ashford ←" into grass.
5. **Interiors that look alike:** all inns and taverns use one template.

Also minor: in the snowy north there is still a parallel double road (`b2_37`), Greyhelm's south row reads as hedges, and passing out at 02:00 cost 2,500g with no inn in reach.
