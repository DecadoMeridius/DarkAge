# Art & feel playtest, round 2: farm, village, tavern (cozy-game critic)

Persona: a Stardew Valley fan judging coziness, life and art. New game ("Willa", ponytail, green shirt), one full day: Mon 1, 6:00 to 00:40, then Tue 2 morning.
Route: tilled, planted and watered 2 turnips → walked to the village at 7:00 → plaza in the morning → Mara's store (bought tulip bulbs) → plaza around 10:30 → Drowsy Drake at 11:20 (closed until 12) and at lunch (12:40-14:00) → plaza at 15:30, 17:20, 19:10 (golden hour) and 20:50 (dusk) → Drowsy Drake at 21:20 → walked home at night → slept in the tent → rainy Tue morning.
Screenshots are in the harness scratchpad, named `b3_<n>.png`.

## Summary: cozy and alive **7/10** (up from 6)

The village now feels inhabited. By mid-morning the plaza has 5-7 people walking around. The evening tavern is the best scene in the game so far: about 10 people, a crackling fireplace, lit lamps, Brann and Hale having a drink. The villagers' one-liners have real charm. Three things hold it back from an 8:
- **Talking to the new folk is broken.** The greeting says `"undefined"`, Chat says `"undefined"`, and their real line hides behind a button labelled **Shop**.
- **Lit windows are drawn over the player at night.** A glowing square sits on your face whenever you stand in front of a building.
- **People stand instead of sitting**, and they clip into you and into props.

The art itself is close to Stardew quality and mostly consistent.

## What improved
- **Street life by day.** At 7:10 only Brann and Mara were out (b3_8). By 8:20 there were 4 (b3_16), by 10:40 6-7 around the fountain (b3_27) and by 11:00 5+ near the tavern (b3_29). People walk with purpose, stop and turn, and drift into shops when they open (Brann was gone into the smithy after 9:00). That is a big step up from "empty at noon".
- **Townsfolk have personalities.** Nell the washerwoman: "Pip waters the ale. Don't tell him I said." Gil the tinker: "If you see a goat on the roof, it's Odile's." Maud: "Brann works that forge from dawn till supper. Then he works the tavern." Dunstan in the tavern: "Don't play dice with the miller." These are exactly Stardew-flavoured, and Maud's line even sets up the evening scene.
- **The evening tavern** (b3_45) is packed and warm: Pip behind the bar, two patrons leaning on it, Brann on the rug, people at the table and on the bench, the fire glowing, candles lit. This is the cozy moment the game needed.
- **Lights at night.** Lamp posts are dark by day and lit at night, and windows glow (b3_43, b3_49). Mara's big shop windows glowing amber at 23:30 is lovely.
- **The see-through ghost** only appeared when something really covered me (behind the bar, b3_37/38; standing on Hale, b3_46). I never saw it on open ground.
- **Small touches:** chimney smoke, the tent's "smells of thyme, like her old house" (b3_52), the morning report announcing rain, a story and the stray (b3_53), and rain streaks on the farm (b3_54).

## Art

**Lovely**
- Building exteriors: the tavern's half-timber with dormers, the stone smithy, Odile's board-and-batten stables and the Mara's store awning (b3_11, b3_24, b3_29). These are Stardew-grade.
- Interiors: the store's shelves, plant, rug and lamp (b3_25), and the tavern's fireplace, casks, banner and painting (b3_33).
- The fountain, the well and the title screen.
- The night grade with glowing windows is moody and readable.

**Off-style / broken**
1. **Night window glow covers the player** (b3_43, b3_44). The amber window square is drawn after the sprites, so it pastes over Willa's head. It's the most visible mood-breaker of the evening.
2. **The plaza is a huge flat sea of one cobble texture** (b3_13, b3_15, b3_47). Stardew's town breaks paving with planters, benches, trees, bins, stalls and flower beds. Here the south half and the east of the plaza are bare stone with one tiny dirt patch. At night it reads as an empty blue carpet.
3. **Golden hour is too orange on cobbles** (b3_42). The plaza turns terracotta-saturated, and the grass goes olive.
4. **The ruined greenhouse at night glows bright periwinkle** (b3_51, b3_52). Its glass panes ignore the night grade more than anything else, so it pops out of the dark scene.
5. **Interior windows show daylight at night.** The tavern's back window is sky-blue at 21:20 (b3_45).
6. **Farm debris is laid out on a near-regular grid** (b3_4, b3_6, b3_54). Rows of identical twigs, stones and weeds at even spacing look stamped, not natural. Stardew clusters them.
7. **Character clipping:** Nell walked into and stood on the player (b3_22/23); a villager stood behind and inside the notice board (b3_42); the old man overlapped the player (b3_42); Dunstan and Hale were stood on (b3_35, b3_46).
8. **Maud is labelled "Grandmother" but drawn young and red-haired** (b3_40).
9. **The creator window** is huge with half of it blank (b3_2).

## Atmosphere
- Day: pleasant, with soft ambience and people moving. It finally feels like a village.
- Lunch: flat. The tavern opens at noon and only 2 people show up, and they stand rather than sit.
- Evening: the best part, a full tavern. But nobody sits on the stools or bench; they stand next to them, so the room reads as "crowd waiting" rather than "people relaxing".
- Night walk home: atmospheric. The empty plaza and lone lamps feel peaceful. But the lamps cast no pool of light (by design), so the plaza is very dark-blue and uniform.
- Small friction that breaks the spell:
  - RMB on a walking villager usually misses: they walk on and you arrive at an empty spot (b3_17, b3_19). It took 2-3 tries per person.
  - The hint says "LMB Water" on village grass and on the tavern floorboards (b3_30, b3_34).
  - Brann in the tavern at 21:40 opens the full smithy shop window instead of a chat.
  - Pip can't be reached across the crowd at the bar ("You can't reach that"), and I could walk behind the bar into the bookshelves (b3_37).
  - Esc after a toast opened the Satchel's Options tab instead of doing nothing (b3_14).
  - The arrival door doesn't open on RMB arrival; you need an extra E.
- Chat lines from shopkeepers are mostly gameplay tips: Brann "Bring armor", Mara "Seeds are seasonal", Odile "Pet your animals daily". They read like a tutorial rather than people. The new townsfolk are far more charming than the main cast.

## Concrete ideas, ranked
1. **Fix the townsfolk greeting window.** Use their line as the greeting, make Chat cycle their lines, and drop the "Shop" button for people with no shop. Right now every new villager says "undefined".
2. **Draw night window glow under characters.** Paint it into the building sprite layer, or draw it before entities, so it never covers the player or NPCs.
3. **Seat people.** Patrons on stools and the bench (a sit pose, already partly there with `ride`), bar leaners with a mug. Also give the lunch crowd 4-6 people.
4. **Dress the plaza:** benches, flower planters and tubs, a tree with a round bench, crates and barrels by shop doors, a market cart, potted plants under windows, some worn/grass-gap tiles. Break the cobble with a few paths and a grassy island.
5. **NPC collision/personal space:** don't let walkers end up on the player or inside props, and make RMB on a walker "lock" them (they stop as soon as you click, not on arrival).
6. **Give shopkeepers personal chat lines** (gossip, weather, feelings) in their Chat cycle, and keep tips for 1 in 4.
7. **Tone down the golden-hour grade on stone** and keep the greenhouse glass under the night grade. Tint interior windows by time of day.
8. **Scatter farm debris in clusters** with varied spacing.
9. **Small fixes:**
   - Maud's age/look.
   - Context hint shouldn't say Water on non-farm ground.
   - Brann in the tavern should chat, not open the forge.
   - Block walking behind the bar.
